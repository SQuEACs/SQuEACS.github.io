Open Science / Tadpole Trek

Tadpole Trek

Guide one tadpole from hatchling to froglet through a polluted pond. Swim out of heavy metals, PFAS, and litter toward clean habitat, eat algae, and see how your path shapes the developing brain.

STAGE 1 Hatchling
Energy

Left thumb: swim · Right thumb: swipe to look

Touch: left thumb to swim, right thumb to swipe and look, plus Up and Dive buttons. Keyboard: arrows or WASD to swim · Space to swim up · Shift or E to dive. Each stage ends when you finish its three goals (or tap End stage). Watch the three exposure meters: the longer your tadpole stays in a plume, the more it fills. Tap the menu slots, or press J, L, K, V, O, X, or T, to open your Journal, Life story, Anatomy, Growth chart, Wardrobe, Pond-dex, and Treasure chest. Opening a menu pauses the game.

Journal

Science journal

How pollution reaches the developing brain in this game
  1. 1Exposure
  2. 2Dose and time
  3. 3Critical window
  4. 4Brain regions
  5. 5Food and growth

Heavy metals and PFAS fill an exposure meter while your tadpole swims in them, and the meter shrinks brain regions that are still forming. Plastic and paper don't touch the brain. They crowd out algae, so your tadpole eats less and grows slower.

Treasure chest

Shiny bubbles drift up during stages. Grab them before they pop. Rare ones restore a heart.

Nature finds

0 of 8

Each stage hides green rings with pond plants and animals inside. They don't pop. Some sit right at the edge of a plume, so choose your route.

The pollutant effects, brain scores, growth values, and anatomy drawings in this game are illustrative, not measurements. Real exposure depends on chemical, dose, species, and timing, and the hypotheses on the cards are ideas our lab and others are testing, not settled answers. See exactly how the game works →
