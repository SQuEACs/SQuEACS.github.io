NoCTURN · Non-Clinical Tomography Users Research Network

Dr. Lynch chairs the Conference and Digital Coordination Committee of the Non-Clinical Tomography Users Research Network (NSF award 2226186, 2022–present). NoCTURN builds shared standards and open access for CT data across science, engineering, and education.
