Mammal Mystery

A detective game for pre-K to 2nd grade. Detective Ollie leads an expedition through habitats. Kids follow the clues to find animals hiding behind bushes and rocks, discover each animal's superpower, and collect stickers as they rank up. No reading needed: turn the sound on.

How to play: pick your grade and tap the map to go on an expedition. Listen to each clue and tap the hiding spot of the animal that matches. Wrong picks just pop up and bring another clue. Then tap the glowing body part to see the animal's superpower, and collect stickers to rank up. Tap the speaker to hear anything again. Works on tablets, phones, and computers.

What each grade learns

  • Pre-K: name 12 mammals from simple look clues and shadows, and tap-to-color any animal.
  • K: body parts and what they do, with a "tap my ears" check, and coloring that names each body part.
  • 1st: habitats, and how body parts fit an animal's home.
  • 2nd: teeth and diet (carnivore, herbivore, omnivore), and "Family secrets," a first look at family trees.

The animals

Seven carnivorans (river otter, raccoon, red fox, lion, polar bear, giant panda, meerkat) and five other mammals (rabbit, kangaroo, elephant, giraffe, camel) from six continents. The SQuEACs Lab studies carnivoran anatomy, including river otter skulls, brains, and limbs.

The drawings are original cartoons, simplified for young children. Find all 12 animals to unlock Level 2: 12 surprising mammals, from the egg-laying platypus to the scaly pangolin. The family tree shows widely accepted relationships among the first 12 animals; real mammal family trees have many more branches. Progress is saved only in this browser.
