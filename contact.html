Students

We take on Midwestern University students from the biomedical sciences, osteopathic, veterinary, and pharmacy programs. Students in the lab segment CT scans, collect morphometric and genomic data, and present their work at meetings such as SICB and the American Association for Anatomy.

Email Dr. Lynch with your program, your research interests, and when you can start.

Collaborators

We welcome collaborations on CT imaging, museum specimens, comparative anatomy, and morphological evolution, including shared use of our 3D data.

Email the lab or Dr. Lynch to start a conversation.
