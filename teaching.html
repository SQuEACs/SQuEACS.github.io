Teaching anatomy

Dr. Lynch teaches Gross Anatomy/Embryology at Midwestern University, which serves six clinical programs: osteopathic medicine, physician assistant studies, nurse anesthesia, occupational therapy, veterinary medicine, and optometry. She has taught graduate anatomy since 2013.
