Skull Detective

A CT scan science game for kindergarten to 6th grade. Explore the skulls of five weasel-family cousins and solve a case for your grade: find skull parts, match skulls to habitats, measure them, look inside at the brain, read the teeth, and build the family tree. Everything is read aloud.

Teachers: press and hold the gear on the title screen for settings (lock a grade, slower voice, captions, clear progress).

How to play: pick your grade and open the case. Each grade has its own mission: find skull parts (K), sort skulls by job (1st), match skulls to habitats (2nd), measure skulls (3rd), look at the brain inside (4th), read the teeth and follow the energy (5th), and build the family tree (6th). Everything is read aloud and shown as captions. Tap the speaker to hear it again. Works with touch, a mouse, or a keyboard (Tab and Enter).
