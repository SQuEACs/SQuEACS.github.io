Open Science / Inside the Tadpole

Inside the Tadpole

A CT scan science game for kindergarten to 6th grade. Peek inside a tadpole and a fish, slice through them the way a CT scanner does, and complete a mission for your grade: find body parts, follow the life cycle, learn how messages travel from the senses to the brain and muscles, follow energy up a food chain, and learn the body systems. Everything is read aloud.

How to play: pick your grade, meet the animals, and start the mission. Tap any highlighted science word to see and hear what it means. Tap body parts on the see-through tadpole, put stages in order, and move the slice slider from head to tail to see inside, the way a CT scanner does. Everything is read aloud and shown as captions. Tap the speaker to hear it again. Works with touch, a mouse, or a keyboard (Tab, Enter, and arrow keys for sliders).

What each grade learns

  • K: body parts and what they do (K-LS1-1).
  • 1st: the frog life cycle, and young animals compared with their parents (1-LS3-1).
  • 2nd: what a tadpole needs to survive in its pond habitat (2-LS4-1).
  • 3rd: how CT scans work, structures and their functions, and life cycles (3-LS1-1).
  • 4th: how messages travel from a sense organ to the brain and back to a muscle (4-LS1-1, 4-LS1-2).
  • 5th: the intestine, diet, and how energy moves up a food chain (5-PS3-1, 5-LS2-1).
  • 6th: levels of organization, body systems, and how they work together (MS-LS1-3).

The scans

The SQuEACs Lab stains tadpoles and fish with iodine before CT scanning, so soft tissues like the brain, gut, gills, and muscles show up, not just bone. Scanners image the body in thin slices, which this game lets kids slide through.

For teachers

The classroom edition lets you lock a grade, slow the voice, and turn captions on or off. The teacher guide has a lesson plan for each grade.

Teacher guide

The tadpole and fish are simplified drawings based on the lab's stained CT scans; real scan slices will appear beside them. Progress is saved only in this browser.
