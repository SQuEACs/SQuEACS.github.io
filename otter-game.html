Open Science / Otter Evolution

Otter Evolution

Guide a population of river otters through eight generations. Play each season as one otter, then watch natural selection, inheritance, mutation, and luck reshape the whole population. Pick snowy, desert, fast, slow, murky, or deep-lake homes, and see how bodies, brains, and inner ears evolve to match.

GEN 1 Deep Clear Lake Food 0/8

Keyboard: ← → move · ↑ or Space jump and surface · ↓ dive. Eat fish and crayfish, dodge eagles and logs, and find 8 food to complete each season's quest.

Each new kind of pup your population produces joins your collection. Different habitats evolve different otters, so explore them all.

The habitat targets, trait values, and anatomy drawings in this game are illustrative, not measurements. Real populations evolve over many more generations, and the hypotheses on the cards are ideas our lab and others are testing, not settled answers.
