Open Science / Otter Evolution

Otter Evolution

Guide a population of river otters through eight generations. Explore a small 3D world as one otter each season, escape predators, and finish goals. Then watch natural selection, inheritance, mutation, and luck reshape the whole population. Pick snowy, desert, fast, slow, murky, or deep-lake homes, and see how bodies, brains, and inner ears evolve to match.

GEN 1 Deep Clear Lake
Energy

Left thumb: move · Right thumb: swipe to look

Touch: left thumb to move, right thumb to swipe and look, plus Jump and Dive buttons. Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move · Space to jump and swim up · Shift or E to dive. Each season lasts until you finish its three goals (or tap End season), with predators to escape and an energy bar to keep full. Use the minimap to find the yellow landmark. Tap the menu slots, or press J, L, K, V, O, X, or T, to open your Journal, Life story, Anatomy, Evolution chart, Wardrobe, Otter-dex, and Treasure chest. Opening a menu pauses the game.
